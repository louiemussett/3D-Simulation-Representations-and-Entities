Yes. In the current code, an **ecological year is explicitly defined as 120 days**:

```js
export const DAYS_PER_ECOLOGICAL_YEAR = 120;
```

So my previous timing calculations were based on the simulation’s own compressed calendar, not a 365-day year.

That means:

- 1 ecological year = **120 simulated days**
    
- 1 ecological month = **30 days**
    
- therefore 1 ecological year = **4 ecological months**
    

This is a simulation design choice, not a real-world calendar.

If you intended a year to mean **365 days**, then the current clock system is compressed and would need changing.
