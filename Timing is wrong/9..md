# My conclusion after examining it

The 120-day year was doing **two different things simultaneously**.

It was:

**1. Compressing the calendar for observation/gameplay.**

But it was also accidentally functioning as:

**2. A biological scaling system.**

That is why some of your current values look strangely plausible relative to a 120-day year.

For large herbivores in particular:

> **current gestation / 120**

often resembles:

> **real gestation / 365**

surprisingly well.

But other organisms—especially canids and cats—already use pregnancy durations closer to real days, while their maturation and lifespan are still heavily compressed.

So your biological timing currently isn't governed by one consistent principle.

The most scientifically robust solution would be:

> **Change the ecological calendar to 365 days, then perform a species-by-species life-history calibration rather than multiplying everything by 3.04.**

I would preserve literal-hour/day processes such as metabolism, estrous-cycle durations and many short-term physiological processes.

I would recalibrate:

- `matureAge`
    
- `oldAge`
    
- `maxAge`
    
- `gestation`
    
- `dependency`
    
- lactation/postpartum relationships
    
- reproductive season
    
- litter interval
    
- species-specific estrous cycle
    
- egg incubation for oviparous species
    

against each species' intended real-world analogue.

And critically, I would stop having ecologically unrelated species inherit the same generic **80-day maturity / 65-day gestation / 360-day lifespan** unless those numbers happen to be appropriate.

That would substantially increase the biological realism of the simulation—not just its calendar realism.

The biggest finding is therefore: **365 days is the right calendar if Earth-like animal biology is the intended basis, but changing the year alone is only about one-third of the job. The current animal timing data need a systematic life-history audit because some values were scaled to the old year and others appear to be literal or gameplay-compressed days.**
