Selected-animal example: the one thick dark panel, local colour border and tether keep every channel attached to this animal. The public lower row reads expression → identity/sex/life stage/pregnancy → communication/action. Every admitted animal uses this same card design; a wheel event samples camera-to-animal distance and continuously scales the complete surface. Only the selected animal may add the private upper row: the solid white thought describes its current priority while the dashed forecast describes a separate fallible prediction. An undecided ellipsis or broken forecast ring can briefly occupy those bays when real content is unavailable. Semantic colours still mean danger, water, care and other world cues; ownership never recolours them.

One scroll-scaled ownership card
There are no thin, summary or expanded zoom variants. With no selection, a stable centre-weighted budget admits a small set of thick public cards. Selecting a living animal suppresses every other animal card and nameplate, leaving only that animal's expanded instrument; deselection restores the strategic budget. Cinema follows the same exclusive rule but nominates its own camera-primary subject without granting private observer selection, and subjectless world shots show none. On a wheel event, the active card samples distance to its own animal—not the ground—and stores one continuous bounded scale until the next wheel event. Camera movement changes viewport visibility, not panel size.
Two selected-only private clouds
The solid white thought cloud is a Laboratory rendering of the selected animal's current priority: what matters now, not a prediction. If no priority has committed, a branching ⋯ UNDECIDED symbol pulses instead of inventing a motive. A dashed pale-cyan FORECAST cloud may appear beside it when a meaningful admitted forecast becomes eligible. It uses uncertain wording such as “possible danger”; threat forecasts label estimated event chance, while other families label a model-confidence band. If none qualifies, a broken ◌? FORECAST NOT READY or NO QUALIFIED FORECAST status pulses. If a qualified estimate has not materially changed, the same ring says FORECAST UNCHANGED rather than pretending it failed. These statuses describe the prediction process—they never mean safe, no predator, no water or no event. Hover, Laboratory access or Cinema interest cannot place either private cloud above an unselected animal. Neither is a public signal, invented inner speech or verified world fact.
Type-specific transition timing
Expressions, callouts and ordinary thoughts use semantic profiles between 0.5 and 5 seconds rather than one global hold. Text-bearing private forecasts remain for 3 to 8 seconds; empty forecast pulses vary from 3 to 5 seconds before a 1.75-second quiet phase. Each profile has an entry delay, minimum readable time, release grace and interruption priority. A sudden startle lasts 0.5 seconds; settled safe rest must remain eligible for 1.5 seconds before appearing and receives a 5-second minimum. Fast changes are coalesced to the newest eligible replacement, while urgent danger can interrupt after the universal 0.5-second readability floor. The animal's physiology, decisions, actions, learning and narration evidence continue immediately. Empty thought uses a 1.25-second visible, 1.75-second quiet heartbeat. Selection, viewport or privacy loss, a Cinema owner cut and animal removal clear ineligible artwork immediately.
Action badge
Remains visible while a notable behaviour is actively being performed, then disappears when that behaviour ends or an urgent symbol takes precedence.
Call or signal
A public badge stays attached to its speaker and uses a 0.75–4 second type-specific hold: immediate impact warnings are short, while directional resource reports remain longer. A genuinely new occurrence of the same call can rearm its timer. Sound arcs are not held: they appear only while the animal is actually vocalising. A receiver link appears only when retained observational evidence confirms receipt; an intended recipient alone is not proof that the call was heard. Urgent cues take precedence over ordinary badges.
Identity centre
The middle of the card keeps a short entity ID with sex and maturity markers: B baby, J juvenile, YA young adult, A adult and O old. P and any pregnancy progress shown there are observer-only diagnostic state, not a public announcement or knowledge automatically available to another animal. A local shape/accent and tether pattern redundantly identify ownership in a crowded visible cluster; they do not change the animal or need to be globally unique.
Metabolic reserves
Selected-organism diagnostic showing gut nutrients, blood/liver fuel (blood glucose and liver glycogen), body fat and hydration. When Performance fuels is also visible, it forms the attached upper half of one physiology stack. Its independent Overlays checkbox can still hide only this half; if shown alone, it occupies the common stack position without an empty gap.
Performance fuels
Selected-organism diagnostic showing aerobic endurance, muscle glycogen, adrenaline capacity and accumulated adrenaline stress. Adrenaline mobilises fuel; it is not itself an energy reserve. It forms the attached lower half of the physiology stack when Metabolic reserves is visible, but its independent Overlays checkbox still controls it separately.
Health
Appears when health or maximum recoverable health is below full.
Rings, arrows and links
Most long colour-matched patterned lines are ownership tethers: each joins one card to its animal. White shows facing; the short intent arrow uses the current priority colour. Other separately licensed lines can show a decision cause, recent movement, a recorded actor-to-target action, or confirmed receipt of a call. They may coexist because they answer different questions; none is a prediction path or a permanent social bond.
