# Updated founder-derived species

The species catalogue now contains 28 independently selectable entries.

## Preserved originals

- `grazer` — Valley Grazer
- `hunter` — Ridge Hunter

These two entries retain their original bespoke geometry, colours, proportions, eye placement, movement presentation, and default inclusion. Their catalogue visual recipes remain `null`, ensuring they continue through the original rendering branches.

## Updated alternatives

- `valley-grazer-updated` — Valley Grazer — Updated
- `ridge-hunter-updated` — Ridge Hunter — Updated

The updated models use visible, layered iris-and-pupil eyes and species-readable
outer and inner ear shapes. These additions belong only to the newer visual
recipes; the two original founder models retain their frozen geometry and face.

The updated Valley Grazer is a red-deer-informed medium herbivore with a current two-mass silhouette, visible ears, antlers and tail, revised paired-sensor biology, mixed grazing/browsing ecology, seasonal herd behaviour, explicit terrain profile, and independent life-history and metabolism records.

The updated Ridge Hunter is a grey-wolf-informed pursuit carnivore with a sloped body, pointed head, visible ears and tail, stronger scent and directional hearing, stable-pack social biology, cooperative pursuit ecology, and independent movement, terrain, life-history and metabolism records.

## World setup

The New World panel provides separate presets:

- **Original generics — 2 species** uses only the untouched originals.
- **Updated Valley Grazer & Ridge Hunter — 2 species** uses only the updated alternatives.
- **Vast world — 28 species** includes both original and updated models alongside the other 24 catalogue species.

Custom species controls list all four independently. Existing saves and setups that contain only `grazer` and `hunter` remain valid and do not acquire the updated alternatives automatically.
