# Species colour-photo references

Each link opens a species article whose lead image is the minimum colour-photo reference for modelling. The two generic animals use their stated real-life averages. These references guide silhouette, colour blocking, eye contrast, characteristic markings and relative proportions; they are not runtime assets.
