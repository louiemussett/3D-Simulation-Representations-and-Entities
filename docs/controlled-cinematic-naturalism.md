# Controlled Cinematic Naturalism

Controlled Cinematic Naturalism is clear natural-history narration in which drama comes from the selection, sequence and contrast of verified developments, not invented details.

## Division of work

1. The simulation establishes authoritative facts.
2. Deterministic JavaScript selects facts, records their time, source, subjects, uncertainty and epistemic status, and renders a controlled factual account.
3. The generic browser narrator reports immediate events.
4. One local-model call connects several developments into a chapter overview.
5. The validator approves supported claims or substitutes the deterministic controlled account.

The local model does not rewrite individual shots and is not asked to imitate a named presenter.

## Controlled factual layer

- Use one claim per sentence.
- Name an explicit subject where ambiguity is possible.
- Retain stable terminology and entity identity.
- Record whether information is visually observable, measured by the simulation, remembered from a verified event, scientifically inferred, uncertain, or verified as animal perception.
- Do not convert probability into certainty.
- Do not treat simulation world units as physical measurements unless a conversion is configured.
- Keep observations, interpretations and predictions separate.

## Spoken overview

- Prefer familiar words and active voice.
- Connect earlier state, developments, the present situation and unresolved evidence.
- Create tension through factual order and contrast.
- Use varied, speakable sentence lengths.
- Avoid clichés, constant catastrophe language and unsupported causal framing.
- Never invent emotion, intention, knowledge, measurements, causes or outcomes.
- Permit silence when the overview would only repeat immediate narration.

The chapter packet, controlled account, model result, validation result and fallback state are retained in the documentary timeline for audit.
