# Predictive Minds and Cinema Mode — Draft Implementation Record

Status: Draft. AI-generated implementation; human scientific and programme review required before approval.

## Implemented vertical slice

- Shared immutable prediction contracts define framework, target, referent, horizon, applicability, evidence, output shape, confidence, cost, evaluation, abstention and bounded authority.
- Each animal owns a versioned cognition state, local evidence snapshot, bounded ledger, calibration values, activation state and non-executing structural proposals.
- Hydration/body, remembered-water, observed-motion, hidden-threat and forward-action processes run after sensing. They cannot inspect authoritative entities or resources outside the animal's supplied observation and memory.
- `LEGACY`, `PREDICTIVE_SHADOW` and `PREDICTIVE_ACTIVE` preserve rollback. Shadow predictions cannot alter candidate scores. Active predictions provide bounded score adjustments and physical-feasibility vetoes before existing commitment logic.
- Fixed, adaptive and ablation experiment profiles plus deterministic disruption fixtures support comparison without changing world mechanics.
- ACSS V3 models declare heterogeneous frameworks, output shapes and authority. Forecasts record activation, admission, coordination and authority; the ledger records execution and bounded error attribution.
- Only registered camera-validity safety models may receive documentary veto authority. Existing ecological, audience, story and production learning channels remain separate.

## Main Laboratory interface

The Main Laboratory contains a dedicated **Predictive systems** workspace. It is intentionally exhaustive and is not shown in Mini Laboratory or copied into world overlays.

- The interface is observational, not a cognition-mode control panel. Every animal automatically moves between effective `LEGACY`, `PREDICTIVE_SHADOW` and `PREDICTIVE_ACTIVE` outcomes per decision cycle: legacy is the empty/failed-coalition fallback, shadow holds informative but unqualified predictions, and active admits a sufficiently confident forward-action prediction into bounded scoring.
- Automatic computational attention increases the predictor budget during danger and resource pressure, uses a moderate budget for ordinary decisions, and reduces safe-rest work to the cheapest relevant process. Dormant models consume no prediction budget.
- Population summaries report cycles, admissions, abstentions, corrections, computation cost, structural proposals, active frameworks and authority distribution.
- The selected-animal view shows the epistemic-layer flow, activation/admission/coordination/authority table, complete predictions and outputs, local evidence, priors, learned parameters, calibration, bounded ledger, observed outcomes, correction attribution, structural proposals and serialized cognition state.
- The Cinema view shows the ACSS mode, coalition lifecycle, authority assignments, safety vetoes, complete model catalogue, dependency graph, forecasts, ledger health, learning profile, recent learning and the complete diagnostic snapshot.
- The contract reference defines every framework, authority, abstention state and epistemic layer used by the interface.
