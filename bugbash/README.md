# Bug-bash workspace

This folder is the operational record for stabilising the Living Laboratory prototype.

- [Design baseline](DESIGN-AND-BUG-BASH-BASELINE.md): intended design, feature inventory and acceptance reference.
- [Bug-bash charter](BUG-BASH-CHARTER.md): process, evidence requirements and severity rules.
- [Issue ledger](ISSUE-LEDGER.md): every discovered defect and its state.
- [Change log](CHANGE-LOG.md): intentional fixes, decisions and verification notes.

The goal is not to freeze experimentation. The goal is to make deliberate changes, preserve evidence and prevent repairs from silently undoing intended behaviour.
